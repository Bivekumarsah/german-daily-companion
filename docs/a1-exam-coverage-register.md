# DeutschDaily A1 — Goethe-aligned coverage register

Updated: 2026-10-29. **This is a transparent coverage map and enhancement plan, not a certification or complete Goethe word-list concordance.** All new quiz texts and sample dialogues are original to DeutschDaily.

## Official examination reference (adult Start Deutsch 1)
Goethe-Institut's public descriptions:
- [Goethe A1 adult examination](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/gzsd1.cfm): Hören ~20 min, Lesen ~25 min, Schreiben ~20 min, Sprechen group examination ~15 min.
- [Official free exam-training examples and original audio](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/prf/ueb/pa1.html): this is the authoritative way to check task delivery and authentic audio.
- [Official adult A1 word list](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/en/A1_SD1_Wortliste_02.pdf): should be used to perform a *manual, editorially reviewed* vocabulary concordance. Inclusion in DeutschDaily is NOT currently certified.

## Genuine coverage gain in this release
- Pre-existing course has **260 vocabulary entries across 36 lessons**, with **244 distinct case-folded German strings**.
- Added **100 original bilingual noun flashcard entries in 10 everyday packs**, with German article, English/Nepali meaning, optional German plural and TTS.
- A case-insensitive comparison found **40 items overlap the original 244**. That means **60 newly added unique entries**, for **304 distinct German entry strings across the two banks**. This is *not* a claim of 304 unique Goethe word-list headwords or full official word-list coverage.
- A supplementary five-question recall mini-challenge is available per theme; the existing spaced-review scheduler remains unchanged.
- Two independently authored exam-practice sets: 15 Hören + 15 Lesen MCQs per set, total **60 automatically checked objective questions**. There are also 2 writing form/message tasks and 3 speaking self-practice scenarios per set.
- Listening scripts are hidden during the objective rehearsal and visible after section submission; **device TTS is not professional audio** and the tests are not officially standardized.
- Writing and speaking **are not automatically marked**; user compares model and checklist, then should obtain assessment from a teacher or speaking partner.

## Editorial findings and unfinished release gates
1. **Official word-list gap audit is outstanding.** The original 244 and the supplementary 100 entries were not mechanically/linguistically matched to every official Goethe A1 word-list headword; spellings, word senses, plural forms, article distribution and coverage should be verified by a German-speaking editor.
2. **Pronunciation audit is outstanding** for all 36 lessons, 100 supplementary words and 12 curated pronunciation tutorials. English-sound/Devanagari hints remain approximate, especially ich /ç/, ö and ü.
3. **Exam simulation is not authoritative.** The two new 15+15 objective practice sets match the number of questions but not necessarily the official task formats, audio production, invigilation or weightings.
4. **Writing and speaking quality needs human evaluation.** Rubrics, model answers and practice recordings are learning scaffolds, not official examiner grades.
5. **Real-browser / device QA not completed by GitHub CI**: test Android Chrome, iOS Safari, desktop keyboard navigation, microphone/audio permission behavior, offline updates and dark/light contrast.
6. **The earlier source PDFs** (1,756-page full notes, 609-page phrase notes, adjective/irregular-verb/plural/separable-verb/accusative/dative sheets) are learning references, not a licensed content source. We have not reproduced their slide text and must correct source errors and filter A2+ content during later editorial integration.
7. **Target 48-lesson structure remains a proposal**, not implemented. Future additions should retain existing 36 unit IDs and saved progress.

## Responsible readiness recommendation
- Work systematically through 36 current lessons and supplementary topic vocabulary.
- Attempt both independent mock sets to identify listening/reading weaknesses.
- Complete the Goethe-Institut's **official** sample training with authentic audio.
- Seek teacher/tutor feedback on writing and group-style speaking before booking.
- Do not predict that students who complete these exercises will pass the Goethe A1 exam.
